# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Uber drivers and couriers accessing safety education through an authenticated in-app WebView. Content authors use Open edX Studio.

## Product Purpose
Test whether a mobile-first, interactive, and gamified safety-learning experience improves voluntary engagement, learning gain, and course-specific behavior relative to the existing experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The draft PRD is the current product authority. The P0 learner treatment includes pathway and lesson progress, resume, an accountability dashboard, points, one progress or streak model, up to three badges, one privacy-safe ranking model, and persistent achievements. The supplied United States Mandatory Sexual Misconduct Education document is the content authority for the first complete course. Reward values and cohort standings remain illustrative. Production APIs, identity, measurement, approved media, and final ranking semantics are unresolved.

## Brand Commitments
Extend the existing Uber component kit and Figma-derived tokens. Mobile-first and restrained, using Uber safety blue and positive green to make mastery and progress legible without trivializing sensitive content.

## Evidence on Hand
The latest PRD, the complete United States mandatory course document, supporting proposal PDFs, the component kit, gallery, and lesson templates are available in the workspace. Approved video files and captions are not yet supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard and tap alternatives for drag interactions; labeled controls, visible focus, readable text, reduced motion. Formal WCAG target and supported WebView matrix remain to be agreed.
